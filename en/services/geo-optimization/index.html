AI search is built on regular search

Before answering, ChatGPT search, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity mostly look up pages through search engines and then summarise them. Sites that Google hasn't fully indexed, or that rank far down, rarely get seen by AI either. SEO is the foundation.

AI only cites content it can understand

AI crawlers respect robots.txt and mostly don't run JavaScript. Pages with clear information, structured data and direct answers to questions are easier to quote correctly.

AI cross-checks sources

Whether your name, address and phone match across your site, Google Business Profile and social accounts, and what reviews and other sites say about you, all affect whether AI is confident enough to recommend you.

These changes improve your chances of being found and cited by Google and AI; no one can guarantee a fixed ranking or an AI recommendation. We test before and after so you can see the actual improvement.

Step 1: fix the SEO foundations

We first fix the technical issues that affect Google indexing and rankings: HTTPS, mobile layout, page speed, titles and descriptions on every page, H1 heading structure, image descriptions, the sitemap, robots.txt and redirects from old URLs. We also set up Google Search Console and GA4 and deal with pages Google hasn't indexed.

Why this comes first: AI search mostly starts from search engine results. If your site isn't fully indexed, breaks on mobile or loads slowly, AI struggles to see you too, so GEO alone has limited effect. With the foundations fixed, every item in step 2 can do its job.

FAQ

01. How is GEO different from SEO? +
SEO focuses on making your site easier to find in search results such as Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on making it easier for AI tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity to read, understand and cite your content. They share the same foundations, such as clear page structure, structured data and correct site settings, so they are usually reviewed together.
02. Does GEO guarantee that AI will recommend my business? +
No. Which sites an AI draws on, and whether it mentions you, depends on each AI's algorithms and data sources; no one can promise a position or a citation. What we can do is fix every factor that affects how AI reads and understands your site, improving your chances of being cited, and show you the before-and-after difference in a report.
03. Can I check my website's AI visibility myself first? +
Yes. Our free GEO Check shows, for any URL, whether AI crawlers are blocked, whether there is structured data and how easy the content is to cite, with scores and suggestions. If the score is low, we can help you improve it.
04. How long does this service take? +
The full GEO bundle takes about 7–14 days; fixing SEO foundations first adds about 7 days. Actual timing depends on your site and number of pages.
05. How do payments and deposits work? +
Payment is split in two: a 30% deposit when the contract is signed, and the remaining 70% after the work is completed and approved.
06. Can I do GEO if my site's SEO is poor? +
You can, but it will work less well. AI search usually starts from search engine results, so if Google hasn't fully indexed your site, or it breaks on mobile or loads slowly, AI will struggle to see you too. If your free check shows an SEO score below 80, we recommend fixing the SEO foundations before GEO; if your site has no HTTPS or mobile version, a rebuild is usually better value.
