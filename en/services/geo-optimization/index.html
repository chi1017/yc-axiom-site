Help AI Find, Understand and Cite You

More and more people look things up with AI, and AI answers draw on website content it can access and understand. If your site blocks AI crawlers, lacks structured data or hides key information where it's hard to parse, AI is less likely to mention you correctly. GEO checks and fixes each factor that affects how AI reads and understands your site.

To be clear: these changes improve your chances of being cited by AI. They cannot guarantee AI recommendations, and no one can guarantee a fixed position. We score your site with our GEO tool, list what's costing points, fix them in order of priority and re-test so you can see the real improvement. You can also try the free GEO Check yourself first.

FAQ

01. How is GEO different from SEO? +
SEO focuses on making your site easier to find in search results such as Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on making it easier for AI tools like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity to read, understand and cite your content. They share the same foundations, such as clear page structure, structured data and correct site settings, so they are usually reviewed together.
02. Does GEO guarantee that AI will recommend my business? +
No. Which sites an AI draws on, and whether it mentions you, depends on each AI's algorithms and data sources; no one can promise a position or a citation. What we can do is fix every factor that affects how AI reads and understands your site, improving your chances of being cited, and show you the before-and-after difference in a report.
03. Can I check my website's AI visibility myself first? +
Yes. Our free GEO Check shows, for any URL, whether AI crawlers are blocked, whether there is structured data and how easy the content is to cite, with scores and suggestions. If the score is low, we can help you improve it.
04. How long does this service take? +
Basic GEO work takes about 7–14 days, depending on your site and number of pages. We'll quote the exact timeline and cost after reviewing your site.
05. How do payments and deposits work? +
Payment is split in two: a 50% deposit when the contract is signed, and the remaining 50% after the work is completed and approved.
