Web design services
and specifications

YC AXIOM's three core services are brand websites, custom multi-page sites and systems and search optimization (SEO + GEO): design, build and launch, all the way to helping customers find you on Google and AI search. E-commerce sites, business data cleaning and market research are under other services. If you'd rather pay a fixed monthly fee and update the content yourself from a computer or phone, see website rental.

Search Optimization (SEO + GEO)

Google Search AI search (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews) Speed optimization Structured data
