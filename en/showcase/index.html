How We Work

  1. 01
    Discovery

    We learn about your brand, customers and goals, then plan pages and features.

  2. 02
    Design

    You review Figma designs online and approve colors, fonts and layout before development.

  3. 03
    Development

    Clean code that loads fast, works well on phones and is easy for Google and AI to read.

  4. 04
    Testing and launch

    Launch after cross-device testing, with a 30-day free warranty and the full source code.

Timelines: one-page brand site about 2–3 weeks, multi-page brand site about 3–5 weeks, store and system sites about 6–8 weeks; rental sites publish as soon as the content is filled in.
