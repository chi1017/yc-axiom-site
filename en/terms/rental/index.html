  • The content fields and sections available are those the editor provides; custom design and extra features are not part of this service and can be quoted separately.

2. Prices and rental period

  • Plans: sites are either one-page (all content on a single page) or multi-page (a homepage plus separate pages such as about, prices, photos, news, FAQ and contact), chosen when you create the site.
  • One-page price: the Self-serve plan, {{yc:terms_single}}; or {{yc:terms_single_year}}. For more than 12 months, each full 12 months is charged at the yearly price and remaining months at the monthly price.
  • Multi-page price: the Self-serve (multi-page) plan, {{yc:terms_multi}}; or {{yc:terms_multi_year}}, calculated the same way as one-page.
  • {{yc:terms_lock_note}}
  • One-page can be upgraded to multi-page at any time; once upgraded, it can't be switched back immediately (see Downgrades below). All your content is kept.
  • Upgrade difference: if you've paid and have days remaining, you pay the price difference for those days. For monthly payment, each day is (multi-page monthly fee − one-page monthly fee) ÷ 30; for 12 months paid up front, each day is (the difference between the two 12-month prices) ÷ 365, rounded to the nearest NT dollar. We publish the multi-page version once we confirm the difference is paid; until then the site stays one-page. From the next period, the multi-page price applies.
  • Advanced features (add-on): available for one-page and multi-page, including a booking calendar, custom code and embeds, and collaboration, for an extra NT${{yc:terms_pro_month}} per month, or NT${{yc:terms_pro_year}} for 12 months paid up front. Adding them mid-term requires paying for the remaining days (monthly: NT${{yc:terms_pro_month}} ÷ 30 per day; 12 months up front: NT${{yc:terms_pro_year}} ÷ 365 per day, rounded to the nearest NT dollar), and they're activated once we confirm payment. Custom code is added to the site only after our review.
  • Downgrades: switching from multi-page back to one-page, or cancelling advanced features, can be requested in the member area and takes effect when the next renewal payment is confirmed; no refund is given for the current period. All content is kept after a downgrade (multiple pages are merged back into one; booking calendar, code and collaborator settings are kept and return if you add the features again).
