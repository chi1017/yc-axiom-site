Templates

Website rental templates

Website rental comes with templates for many industries, each with one-page and multi-page example sites you can open on a computer or phone. Once you rent, replace the name, photos, prices, hours and FAQ with your own; colors, fonts and the homepage layout can all be changed in the editor.

Every industry has multi-page and one-page examples, and the two look different, so have a look at both before you decide; sites marked Style example have the most complete content. Businesses, addresses, phone numbers and content in the examples are fictional, and photos come from Unsplash's free license and CC0 public-domain libraries.
