Website Rental

Want a professional website that's easy to find and looks great on phones, without building or maintaining it yourself? With website rental you sign in and fill in your business details; we publish and maintain the site and run an automatic SEO and AI visibility checkup every month.

Rental includes

  • Ready-made templates and an online editor (many industries, with wording and page names adjusted automatically)
  • Edit on computer or phone: the editor has a phone layout and can be added to your home screen like an app
  • One-page or multi-page; prices, photos, FAQ, news and more are all included at no extra cost
  • Online purchases: up to 24 products, paid online straight into your own ECPay account (you need to apply for your own ECPay merchant account first, which requires personal or company details and ECPay’s review; once approved, enter it in the member area to start taking payments. Until then the site only displays products and customers order by phone or LINE)
  • Add a Google Analytics (GA4) measurement ID to see your traffic
  • 16 built-in color schemes, or pick a main color and the rest is matched
  • Publishing and hosting (default address yourname.pages.dev; we can help connect your own domain)
  • SEO and AI-search-friendly structure: complete HTML, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt
  • Automatic monthly checkups, with scores and suggestions in the member area

You fill in the content; we handle launch and maintenance

A traditional website means discussing requirements, waiting for design, then waiting for development, and small businesses often get stuck on budget and time. Rental does the slowest parts up front: the templates, code and structure that SEO and AI search need are ready. You just fill in your name, hours, menu, photos and FAQ and watch the live preview on the right. When you're happy, press Publish and the live site updates in about 1–2 minutes.

About phone editing, SEO and customization

Edit from your phone. The editor has a phone layout with tabs for look, business, content, features and preview, and photos upload straight from your camera roll. Changing your hours, updating menu prices or posting news takes a moment on your phone, and you can add the editor to your home screen like an app. Long text and advanced settings are easier on a computer.

Sites are complete static HTML that search engines and AI crawlers can read. To be clear, this improves your chances of being found and cited but cannot guarantee rankings or AI recommendations. Rental uses our templates and editor; for a fully custom design or extra features, see custom website services.

How rental works

  1. 01
    Sign in to the member area with Google

    Choose a site address and business name; the system checks availability instantly.

  2. 02
    Fill in the editor

    On computer or phone. Business details, hours, menu, photos and FAQ are saved as a draft automatically; preview live on the right on a computer, or switch to the Preview tab on a phone.

  3. 03
    Accept the rental terms and request launch

    Before your first publish, read and accept the website rental terms; if the terms change, you'll be asked to confirm again before your next publish.

  4. 04
    Transfer payment and send the last five digits

  5. 05
    Update any time and check monthly reports

    To change content, go back to the editor and press Publish. Monthly checkup scores and suggestions appear under My Website, and we email a renewal reminder before expiry.

FAQ

01. How is rental different from buying a custom website? +
A custom website is designed from scratch for you, and the source code and settings are handed over when it's done (a one-time purchase). With rental, you use our ready-made templates and online editor, fill in the content yourself, and we handle launch and maintenance. The templates, code and editor remain ours and are provided for your use during the rental; source code is not handed over. For custom design or extra features, ask for a custom website quote.
02. Do I need to know coding or design? +
No. Sign in to the member area, enter your business name, hours, menu and FAQ in the editor and upload photos, with a live preview on the right. Pick one of 16 built-in color schemes, or choose your own main color and the system matches the rest and keeps text easy to read.
03. Can I edit my website on my phone? +
Yes. The editor has a phone layout: tabs at the bottom switch between look, business, content, features and preview; tap an item to open its settings. Changes save automatically, and tapping Publish at the top right updates the live site. Upload photos straight from your camera roll, and tap any section in the preview to jump to its settings. You can also add the editor to your home screen and open it full-screen like an app (on iPhone, tap Share in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap ⋮ in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen). Long text, uploading fonts or pasting code is easier on a computer.
04. What is my website address? Can I use my own domain? +
We provide a default address like yourname.pages.dev. To use your own domain, contact us and we'll help set it up; you are responsible for registering and renewing the domain and adding a DNS record with your domain provider.
05. How do I pay, and when does the site go live? +
Payment is by bank transfer for now; the transfer details appear under My Website in the member area after you sign in. After transferring, send us the last five digits of your account by LINE or email. We activate the site once payment is confirmed, and the expiry date counts from that confirmation. There are two rental options: one-page {{yc:single_label}}, {{yc:single_year_phrase_sp}}; multi-page {{yc:multi_label}}, {{yc:multi_year_phrase_sp}}. You can upgrade from one-page to multi-page any time. For a booking calendar, custom code or collaboration, add advanced features for +{{yc:pro_month}} a month. Upgrades and add-ons mid-term are prorated for the remaining days.
06. What happens if I don't renew? +
We email you before expiry. After expiry the site is marked overdue but stays online for a while; if it isn't renewed within 7 days, the address shows a maintenance page. Your content and settings are kept while suspended, and the site comes back automatically once payment is confirmed. After 30 days of suspension we'll contact you; your data is never deleted automatically for being overdue.
07. Can a rented website guarantee rankings or AI recommendations? +
No, and no one can. The site is generated to be friendly to search engines and AI (complete HTML content, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt), with an automatic monthly checkup whose score and suggestions appear in the member area. These improve your chances of being found and cited, but results still depend on search and AI algorithms and on your content.
08. Where is the site hosted? Will it be slow? +
Sites are hosted on a global CDN, so pages are served from the location closest to each visitor and load quickly in Taiwan. Pages are generated in advance, with no server processing on each visit, so speed stays consistent.
09. Is there a traffic limit? Will it crash with many visitors? +
There's no traffic limit. Lots of visitors at once won't slow it down, and you're never charged extra for traffic.
10. Is there enough space? Do I need to buy hosting? +
No separate hosting or storage is needed. We manage hosting and space, and photos are automatically compressed for the web, so there's plenty of room for a typical small business. See Website specs above for what fits.
11. Can I upload large photos or photos from my phone? +
Yes. Files up to 5MB each in JPG, PNG, WebP or iPhone HEIC can be uploaded directly; the system resizes and converts them for the web.
12. I already have a domain. Can I use it? +
Yes. Once the site is live, enter your domain under My Website in the member area (we recommend the www form, e.g. www.yourshop.com). The system tells you which DNS record to add where you bought the domain, and HTTPS turns on automatically. The address without www can be redirected to www at your domain provider, or we can help. Only the website settings change, so company email is unaffected; if you're not comfortable doing it, we can set it up for you.
