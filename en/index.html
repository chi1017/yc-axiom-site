FAQ

See pricing & plans →
01. Do I fully own the website and its code after the project? +
Yes. YC AXIOM hands everything over. Once the project is completed and accepted, you receive the full source code, the Figma design files and control of the hosting, with no hidden lock-in contracts.
02. How long does a typical website take to build? +
A single-page landing site usually takes 2–3 weeks; a standard multi-page brand site about 3–5 weeks; larger e-commerce or custom-system sites about 6–8 weeks. Rush projects can be prioritized after review.
03. How do payments and deposits work? +
Payment is split in two: a 50% deposit when the contract is signed and work begins, and the remaining 50% once design and development are finished and the site passes cross-device testing and your approval, right before launch.
04. What if I need changes or technical support after launch? +
Every custom project includes 30 days of free support after launch (bug fixes and small text or image changes). After that, you can choose whether to add a monthly maintenance plan.
05. What is the difference between website rental and a custom website? +
A custom website is designed from scratch for your needs, and the source code and settings are fully handed over to you when it is done. Website rental uses our ready-made templates and online editor: you fill in the content, and we handle publishing and maintenance. The templates and code remain ours and are provided for your use while you subscribe; the source code is not handed over. Templates are available for many industries; one-page {{yc:single_label}}, multi-page {{yc:multi_label}}.
06. How do I start a rental website, and what happens if I don't renew? +
Sign in to the member area, fill in your business details and photos in the editor, and pay by bank transfer; the site goes live once we confirm the payment. We send an email reminder before it expires. After expiry the site stays online for a while; if it is not renewed within 7 days, the address shows a "site under maintenance" page. Your content and settings are kept and come back automatically once payment is confirmed. Your data is never deleted automatically. Read the full website rental guide and FAQ →
07. Can you build member logins, online payments and similar features? +
Yes. The Chinese Win Studio website, for example, combines Google sign-in, ECPay online payments (access opens automatically after payment), members-only content, photo uploads and a teacher back office. These are custom systems that take about 6–8 weeks, quoted according to the features.
08. I haven't decided yet. Can I check my current website first? +
Yes. Our free SEO Check, GEO Check and image compressor need no sign-up. If the results are unclear, the Help Center explains them in plain words, or you can ask us on LINE.
09. Can we work together if I'm not in Taichung? +
Yes. YC AXIOM is based in Taichung and works with clients across Taiwan and abroad; we communicate over LINE, email and video calls.
